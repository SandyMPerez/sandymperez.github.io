Beyond the Resume

I grew up on a barrier island on the east coast of Florida and have always been drawn to water, animals, and travel. I enjoy bike rides around town, kayaking, exploring new parks, going to community events, or taking my dogs for a ride in the car along the river with all of the windows down. I’m always on the lookout for great restaurants with good food, kind service, and a beautiful view.

Over the years I’ve worked with people from all walks of life in roles as varied as dental clinic supervisor, licensed massage therapist & spa manager, and funeral director and embalmer. I've owned two successful businesses, and feel all of these experiences gave me a deep respect for regulated, detail-driven work and for caring for people during some of their most important moments.

Math has always been my favorite subject, and I’ve been analytical for as long as I can remember – I was the kid who took electronic toys apart just to see how they worked and then put them back together. I still own my favorite childhood toy: a Little Professor calculator. That same curiosity now shows up in my love of data, operations, and process improvement. I’m a lifelong learner (friends & family jokingly call me a “professional student”) and I’m constantly taking courses, earning new credentials, and sharpening the skills that help me understand problems, design better systems, and support the people around me.
